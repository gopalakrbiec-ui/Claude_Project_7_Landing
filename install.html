How to install Savi Nenapu

Android 5.0 and above · Beta version

Savi Nenapu is currently in beta and installed directly from an APK file (a normal Android app installer). It takes about a minute. Do this on your Android phone.

Download the APK

Step by step

  1. Download the APK using the button above. Your phone may warn that this type of file can harm your device — this is the standard message for any app installed outside the Play Store. Tap Download anyway / OK.
  2. Open the file. Tap the downloaded savi-nenapu.apk from your notification shade or your Files / Downloads app.
  3. Allow installs from this source. The first time, Android will ask permission. Tap Settings, then turn on Allow from this source (for your browser or Files app), and go back.
  4. Install. Tap Install, wait a few seconds, then tap Open.
  5. Sign in and start creating. Create your account, add credits with UPI, and make your first creation.

Is this safe? Yes. The "unknown source" warning appears for every app not installed from the Play Store — it does not mean anything is wrong. You are installing the official Savi Nenapu app from our own website, savinenapu.in. You can turn the permission back off after installing.

Prefer the Play Store?

You can also join our test on Google Play instead of installing the APK. Join the Play test here. A full Google Play release is on the way.
